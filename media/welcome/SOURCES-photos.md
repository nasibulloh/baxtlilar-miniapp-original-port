# Welcome photo clips: sources and licences

Candidate background clips for the tour (owner, 2026-11-09: «the audience is mostly Uzbek; show Uzbek / Central Asian couples, young and
married many years»). Made from still photos (Ken Burns), because no free Central Asian couple video was found on 2026-11-08
(see public/media/welcome/SOURCES.md). Downloaded 2026-11-09 without an account at 2000 px wide; built by `scripts/build.py`:
crop to 9:16, upscale to 2160x3840, `zoompan` 1.00↔1.08 over 125 frames around the faces, out at 540x960, 25 fps, 5.0 s,
H.264 Main (level 3.1), yuv420p, no audio track, `+faststart`, crf 23 (25 where needed to stay under 700 KB). Each `.jpg` is the
first frame of its `.mp4` (the poster). In the repo since 2026-11-09 as review variants b, c and d of the tour (`?tour=b`, src/features/auth/tour/video.tsx); kept in review/media/welcome so only a mock (demo) build ships them; variant a is the old playlist.
